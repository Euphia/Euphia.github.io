## Speech techniques
Many people become hoarse from misusing their voice when they speak, especially if they speak in an unsuitable manner for extended periods.Even singers who use correct technique and have no problems during singing can become hoarse when speaking.Singers, actors, preachers, schoolteachers, politicians and instructors of sports, aerobics and dance all run the risk of straining the voice.For all groups, knowing the advantages and limitations of the modes can prevent the vocal problems, as hoarseness often arises from not being aware of or using the advantages and disadvantages of the modes.

### Preserve identity
Modifying the way, you speak should be done with great care as altering the voice can feel like changing your identity.It is important to avoid regimentation and smoothing out personal distinctive characters.Minor ‘faults’, such as a lisp, can be endearing, so do not feel obliged to correct them unless you really want to.Changing your pitch for speaking may also feel like changing your identity.I see no reason for doing this unless you are truly unhappy with the pitch.

Changing your voice can cause a lot of vocal problems.There have been cases in which parents have complained to their doctor that they do not like the shrill and loud voice of their child.The doctor has recommended that the child speak in a quieter volume and with a less shrill sound.To be ordered to talk quieter and to change the sound of your voice is not only an enormous psychological strain, it is also potentially straining on the voice.Such a voice often becomes so weak and inaudible that, even many years after, it constitutes a handicap for the owner, not to mention the psychological consequences!

### Speaking in different modes
Generally, speaking differs from singing with regards to vowels and pitch.The way one treats the vowels in singing and in speaking is quite different.In speaking, you spend a shorter time on the vowels.When singing, you do not distinguish between long vowels, short vowels or stressed vowels, as you do in speaking.Furthermore, speaking is usually conducted in the lower part of the voice, where it might seem as though all vowels can be used in the metallic modes.This is because the vocal modes resemble each other here.So, in speaking, we do not usually have to be so specific about which metallic modes we are using; we can make it easier by simply speaking of ‘the metallic modes’.The vowels will determine which modes are being used.

But it makes sense to distinguish between the densities of the modes when speaking.If you want a voice with many nuances and with many forms of expression, it is practical to be able to speak in several different modes and their densities.In general, when you speak loudly, you should use the fuller metallic modes and when you speak in a medium volume you can use the fuller or reduced density metallic modes, and when you speak quietly, you should use Neutral.

Apart from this, you can find people who mainly – or even solely - speak in Curbing, Edge or Neutral.The chosen speaking mode generally indicates the person’s level of energy and temperament.

Many vocal problems appear from not being aware of which mode you use when you speak, and therefore not obeying the rules of the modes.

### A dynamic speaking voice
To obtain a dynamic speaking voice with many nuances and with many volumes, it is practical to be able to change between the modes and their densities when speaking.When you speak quietly, you should use Neutral, when speaking in medium volume you can use all of the metallic modes in fuller and reduced density and when you speak loudly you should only use the fuller metallic modes.Practise changing between the modes and their densities in this way according to the volume.
