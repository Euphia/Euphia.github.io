## 轻柔的音量 quiet volume
To sing in a quiet volume, you have to choose the vocal mode Neutral either with fuller or reduced density, or in falsetto.You can sing in fuller or reduced density Neutral at a quiet or medium quiet volume all the way up your range.Neutral in falsetto gets more and more difficult in the lower part of the voice.So, in the low and very low part of the voice you often sing in fuller or reduced density Neutral when singing quietly.

In all cases, you must obey the rules for Neutral in order to stay in Neutral all through the range.




If you want to sing in fuller density Neutral at a quiet or medium quiet volume all the way up, you must obey the rules for Neutral in fuller density.Be aware that the fuller density often is easier in the lower part of the voice, but it is definitely possible to sing fuller density Neutral in the higher part of the voice.This requires that you can control the fuller density, know the rules and apply a good amount of support energy.

Perhaps you want to sing in reduced density at a quiet or medium quiet volume all the way up.In this case, you must obey the rules for Neutral in reduced density.Be aware that the reduced density often is easier in the higher part of the voice, but it is definitely possible to sing reduced density Neutral in the lower part of the voice.This requires that you can control the reduced density, know the rules and apply a good amount of support energy.



> See ‘Finding reduced density in Neutral (byway Neutral)’


> Exercise at quiet volume, Byway


Perhaps you want to sing in Neutral in falsetto at a quiet or medium quiet volume all the way up.In this case, you must be aware that Neutral in falsetto do not exist in the lower part of the voice.So, in the low and very low part of the voice you must sing in reduced or fuller density Neutral to continue singing in a quiet volume.At a certain point from the middle part of the voice, you can change to Neutral in falsetto.This transition can be difficult to perform without a break.To make this transition easier you can change from fuller to reduced density by adding a hold.Now keep this hold from reduced density to Neutral in falsetto.在这种平衡动态下，可以丝滑转换到假声。Once you have obtained the falsetto you can continue keeping the hold when singing upwards.这些转换通常对技术要求较高，因此往往需要单训练。
