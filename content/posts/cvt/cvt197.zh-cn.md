## The tongue
Sound colour is produced among other places at level 5 (the soft palate, uvula, back wall of the throat and the back of the tongue), when broadening or compressing the tongue.



- See 'Various levels of the vocal tract – sound colour’



## Compressed tongue
If you make the tongue small, the sound colour becomes darker because the vocal tract becomes larger.This is referred to as ‘compressing the tongue’.The tip of the tongue lies flat down in the oral cavity, the tongue arches in the middle and at the back it pulls into itself.With the tongue in this position, the vowels become more classical - this sound is not yet accepted within popular singing.






音频示例 - Compressed tongue in Neutral for women and Edge for men


Find the position of the compressed tongue by trying to sing in a classical style.Maintain this position and feel exactly how the tongue is placed.You can look in a mirror and see that the tongue is formed like a spoon (of course, you cannot see the back of the tongue).In classical singing technique, this position is called ‘cucchiaio’, which is the Italian word for ‘spoon’.

The old Italian singing schools practise this tongue position by placing the back of a spoon on the tongue and then pressing the tongue forward against the spoon.In this way, the tongue is pressed forwards at the back and pressed backwards at the front, which makes it rise in the middle.This is a compressed tongue.Later, the position is practiced without the spoon.

### Compressed tongue through images and sensations
You can also find the compressed tongue through images and sensations by:

- Imagining that you are grabbing something and pulling it in with the tongue while the sides of the tongue are placed against the upper molars
- Imagining having a hot potato in your mouth
