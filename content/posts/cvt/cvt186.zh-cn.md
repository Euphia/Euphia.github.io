### 音色形成于声道 Sound colour is produced in the vocal tract
It is possible to produce healthy sound colours if you always follow the three overall principles of singing, use the modes correctly and control the techniques for the sound colour.The theory behind producing healthy sound colours is to focus primarily on working with the vocal tract.This reduces the risk of straining or damaging the voice.Just like vocal modes, healthy sound colour is made in the vocal tract.

Various levels of the vocal tract – sound colour
To get an overview of where the gestures for the various sound colour options are produced, it may be useful to describe the vocal tract’s various levels.

Sound colour are mainly produced at levels 3-6.All variations of sound colours are produced as a combination of various levels, while obeying the overall principles and the rules of the modes.

第一层次： 是声带层次。

第二层次： 是室带（假声带）层次。

第三层次： 是杓状软骨层次，或称楔形软骨、会厌和杓状会厌襞层次。This is where sound colour is produced, when necessary and distinct twang are created by more or less narrowing in the epiglottic funnel.

第四层次：是下咽和梨状隐窝层次。This is where the sound colour is produced when the larynx lowers or raises.

第五层次：是软腭、悬雍垂、咽喉后壁和舌根层次。This is where the sound colour is produced when raising or relaxing the soft palate, when opening or closing the nasal passages, and when broadening or compressing the tongue.这里是用舌头以各种形态形成元音的地方。

第六层次： 是声道的其余部分。This is where the sound colour is produced when extending or relaxing the corners of the mouth.
