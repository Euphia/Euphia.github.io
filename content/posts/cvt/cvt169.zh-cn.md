## 主元音让歌词更易理解 Main vowels make lyrics understandable
掌握与元音、音量和音高相关的最佳模式非常重要。使用 “金属” 模式时，请注意元音范围的限制，并确保你修改或调整元音以保持在模式中。

你可能觉得修改或调整元音会让歌词听起来不自然，但听众通常已经习惯了这些变化，甚至不会意识到它们的存在。事实上，听众只有在仔细对比单词与语音元音时才会注意到这些差异。从歌手的角度来看，当模式的中心区完善后，调整元音会显得非常自然，并且实际上可以让歌词更加清晰。相反，试图保持语音中的元音可能会妨碍模式的中心区，影响整体音效，使歌词变得难以理解，同时可能触发不必要的音质变化。同时可能触发不必要的音质变化。

### 所有的元音 All vowels
如果你仅按日常说话的方式发音歌词，那么你只能在低音部分演唱。因为在这个音域，模式彼此相似，允许更大的元音选择。如果你希望在高音部分保持语音中的所有元音，则会受到限制，只能使用 Neutral。但即使在中性模式中，高音部分元音的融合也是不可避免的。

### 元音的有限选择
In Curbing, Overdrive and Edge, altering the vowel sounds is a condition for obtaining the modes.In order to obtain Curbing you must change the vowels to the restrained metallic vowels: ‘UH’ (as in ‘hungry’), ‘O’ (as in ‘woman’) or ‘OO’ (as in ‘you).In order to obtain Overdrive, you must change the vowels to the clear metallic vowels: ‘EH’ (as in ‘stay’) and ‘OH’ (as in ‘so’).In order to obtain Edge, change the vowels to the twanged, clear metallic vowels: ‘I’ (as in ‘sit’), ‘EH’ (as in ‘stay’), ‘A’ (as in ‘and’) and ‘OE’ (as in ‘herb’).

Also, the volume plays a part in relation to vowels.All the metallic mode vowels have to be sung in a relatively loud volume (mf-ff) in order to maintain the metallic modes.


## Exact and ‘wider’ metallic main vowels
Some vowel sounds have to be exact for the technique to work, while other vowels can have a wider range of nuances and the technique will still work.You could call these vowels ‘wider’, as they can contain more possibilities within the vowel.

## Curbing
在 Curbing 模式下演唱 'O' 和 'OO' 时，要有非常准确地分辨元音细微差别，使技术行之有效。

元音 'UH' 有“更多”的可能性，因为你可以在元音上做很大的变化，而 Curbing 的技术仍然有效。这是因为 'UH' 涵盖许多元音的微妙听感，许多元音都有 'UH' 音。例如，法语中的 "un"、"don"、"blanc "和 "vin"，或者英语中的 "fear"。"I" 也可以在 UH 中捕捉到。

### Overdrive
用 Overdrive 唱 "EH" 需要精确的元音的微妙听感，使技术行之有效。

元音 "OH"，有 "更多" 的可能性，因为你可以在元音上做很大的变化，而 Overdrive 的技术仍然有效。从 "OH" 调整到 "OE"。

在许多语言中，例如英语，有些元音只能作为双元音存在。双元音可能会给歌手带来问题，因为双元音中的两个元音会触发两种不同的嗓音模式。使用 Overdrive 模式时，你必须唱出双元音的第一个元音，避免唱出第二个元音。

## 以高密态和低密态演唱元音 Vowels sounds in fuller and reduced density
The vowel sounds in Neutral, Overdrive and Edge in reduced density have a more contained character, but apart from this, they are the same vowels that can be used in Neutral, Overdrive and Edge in fuller density,

### 以高密态和低密态演唱元音 Vowels sounds in fuller and reduced density
In this illustration you can see the various vowel sounds in fuller and reduced density in the various modes.The outer circle represents the vowel sounds in fuller density and the inner circle represents the vowel sounds in reduced density.The circle is cut into quarters, one for each mode.The orange quarter represents Curbing.You can see that it only has an inner circle, as Curbing only exists in reduced density and not in fuller density, which is why the outer circle for Curbing is missing.The blue quarter represents Edge.The red quarter represents Overdrive, and the yellow quarter represents Neutral.Both Neutral, Overdrive, and Edge can be sung in both reduced and fuller density.

You can see on the chart that I (as in ‘sit’) can be sung in both Curbing and Edge in the reduced density.And EH (as in ‘stay) can be sung in both Overdrive and Edge in the reduced density.And also, OO (as in ‘you’) can be sung in both Neutral and Curbing in the reduced density.

In the middle of the inner circle you see the vowel ‘UH’ (as in ‘hungry’).This vowel can encompass many vowel nuances, as you see in the inner circle.In Curbing, the ‘UH’ sounds are restrained and have the same character as ‘OO’ and ‘O’.When you want to sing ‘I’, ‘A’, ‘OE’, or ‘EH’ for Edge in reduced density, it is often easier to direct the vowels towards UH to approach the contained character of reduced density Edge.Likewise, if you want to sing the vowels ‘EH’, ‘OE’, or ‘OH’ for Overdrive in reduced density, again you can direct them towards the UH to establish the contained character needed to sing in reduced density Overdrive.And when you want to sing ‘AH’, ‘EE’ or ‘OO’ (or any vowel) for Neutral in reduced density, it is often easier in the beginning to direct the vowels towards ‘UH’ to establish the right position.The vowel sounds in Curbing will have a restrained character and the vowel sounds in Neutral, Overdrive, and Edge with reduced density will have a contained character.

In the outer circle, that represents fuller density, the vowels are clear and open.You can use the chart by practising directing your vowel sound more towards UH the more you reduce the density.And you can practise directing the vowels sounds more towards a clear and open vowel sound the fuller in density you want to sing.Exercising transitions using the vowel nuances is a great way to train smooth transitions between the modes and between the highway and byway.If you experience a break between any setting and vowel, you may have entered Neutral in falsetto

### Directions of the vowel AH in the various modes
The AH vowel is often causing problems as it is only exists in Neutral.Additionally, it is a vowel sound often used in many words and it can be difficult to obtain in the metallic modes unless you know how to change/direct the vowel sound.So, on this illustration you can see the changing of the AH vowel needed for obtaining the vowel in fuller and reduced density in the various modes.

The outer circle represents the vowel sounds in fuller density and the inner circle represents the vowel sounds in reduced density.The circle is cut into quarters, one for each mode.The orange quarter represents Curbing.You can see that it only has an inner circle, as Curbing only exists in reduced density and not in fuller density, which is why the outer circle for Curbing is missing.The blue quarter represents Edge.The red quarter represents Overdrive, and the yellow quarter represents Neutral.Neutral, Overdrive, and Edge can be sung in both reduced and fuller density.

In the middle of the inner circle you see the vowel ‘AH’ (as in ‘far’).

If you want to sing the ‘AH’ vowel in Neutral (the yellow quarter) it is easy, as the vowel exists in Neutral, meaning you do not have to alter the vowel sound.
If you want to sing the ‘AH’ vowel in Curbing (the orange quarter), you have to direct the vowel towards an ‘UH’ (as in ‘hungry’) in order to obtain the mode.
If you want to sing the ‘AH’ vowel in Edge (the blue quarter), you have to direct the vowel towards an ‘A’ (as in ‘and’) in order to obtain the mode.
If you want to sing the ‘AH’ vowel in Overdrive (the red quarter), you have to direct the vowel towards an ‘OH’ (as in ‘so’) in order to obtain the mode.
For reduced density, the vowel sounds in Curbing will have a restrained character and the vowel sounds in Neutral, Overdrive, and Edge will have a contained character.

For fuller density, the vowel changes will be the same as in reduced.But here the vowel sounds for Neutral, Overdrive and Edge will have a clear and open character.

Practise directing your ‘AH’ vowel sound more towards a restrained and contained character the more you reduce the density – and practise making the AH vowel sound clearer and more open the fuller in density you want to sing




### Classical vowels
If you would like to pronounce the vowels as they sound in classical singing, you must compress the tongue.This gives the vowels an ‘operatic’ character, regardless of the mode.If you do not want a classical sound, do not compress the tongue.

### 案例故事 A case story
A well-trained opera singer wanted to perform in musical theatre, but although he auditioned for many, he was never hired.The criticism was that he sounded ‘too much like an opera singer’.Even though he sang in the various modes, he always used the compressed tongue, so the vowels had the same classical sound.We practised many positions for the tongue and completed whole songs with different positions and sounds.Once he realised the importance of the position of the tongue to sound and vowels, he was able to choose from many different sounds.From then on, he never used a compressed tongue in musical theatre auditions, unless the theatre specifically asked for a classical sound.Once he changed the position of the tongue, and thereby the vowels so that they were more natural and less classical sounding, he became successful in obtaining musical theatre roles.
