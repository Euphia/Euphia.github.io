## Speaking in the fuller metallic modes
Almost all men speak in the fuller metallic modes, as does anyone who speaks loudly in the low and middle part of the voice.Those who use the fuller metallic modes are often easy to hear, extroverted, vocally trained people (for instance well-trained actors trained to reach the back row of a theatre without amplification), accustomed to speaking to large crowds or in noisy surroundings.The voice will mostly have a clear and open character, the sound colour can be light or dark, and the volume usually varies from medium loud to very loud.Such people normally have no problems speaking loudly but might have problems speaking quietly.The fuller metallic modes do not work in quiet volumes and consequently the voice fails and creaks if you hold on to this setting in too quiet a volume.



> See 'Creak and Creaking'


Some people speak almost solely in fuller density Edge, which gives a clear, open and sharp sound with a light sound colour.The volume in Edge is medium loud to very loud, meaning that people who speak in Edge will not normally have problems speaking loudly, but may have problems speaking quietly.If sufficient energy and volume are not applied to the Edge mode, it will fail, and the voice will break.The range of voice for people speaking in Edge is usually a little higher in pitch and the volume is loud.This way of speaking is usually effective if you need to speak in very noisy surroundings.

### 案例故事 Case Story
A comedian with many TV appearances had begun rehearsing a large part for the theatre and became hoarse.

He was using Neutral for the part, and although it is an excellent mode for television, it is usually not loud enough for a large theatre.Attempting to make louder volumes in Neutral made him hoarse.

We practised the fuller metallic modes for speaking, as they are much louder.Once he controlled Overdrive and Edge, his hoarseness disappeared.He could obtain the same sound colour as before by shaping his vocal tract as he did in Neutral.He got very excited when he learned that there was a system to change sound colour by changing the size and shape of the vocal tract, because aside from being a comedian, he was also an impersonator.Now he knew what he was doing technically when he created the exact character and sound colour of the voices he mimicked.

### Exercises for speaking in the fuller metallic modes
Locate Overdrive in singing.



> See 'Finding Overdrive'


Remember the tricks to obtain this mode maintain these and speak on the vowels ‘EH’ (as in ‘stay’) and ‘OH’ (as in ‘so’).Keep your voice in a low pitch and a powerful volume to make it easier to stay in the mode.If it is difficult to speak in Overdrive, start singing in Overdrive a little higher where it may be easier to locate.Gradually descend in pitch while ensuring you maintain your Overdrive feeling and the open and clear character until you arrive at your speaking pitch.Change from singing to speaking.Make sure it does not feel uncomfortable or wrong.At first, it may sound a little odd speaking only on two vowels, but as you get used to the metallic modes, you will be able to make a transition from one to the other, and you no longer have to stick to only those two vowels.

Locate Edge in singing.



See 'Finding Edge'


Twang the epiglottic funnel and speak with this sharp, open, clear and loud sound.If you want this sound in speaking, first practise singing Edge as it is easier to find the mode in a higher pitch.Then practise speaking in Edge, keep the twang and use the same procedure as for speaking Overdrive

Make sure that speaking in Edge, as with the other modes, always feels comfortable.



> Exercise for speaking in Edge


> ‘Speaking’ example


Now try to practise other vowels.Practise the Edge vowels ‘I’ (as in ‘sit’), ‘EH’ (as in ‘stay’), ‘A’ (as in ‘and’) and ‘OE’ (as in ‘herb’) as you did with the Overdrive vowels, maintaining the same fuller metallic sound and volume.

Practise placing consonants before and after the vowels, but make sure the vowels do not loose their fuller metallic character.Speak loudly and clearly, but make sure it sounds natural.If it is difficult to locate the fuller metallic modes, start by exaggerating the distinctiveness and the volume.Later, when you are able to speak in the fuller metallic modes, make it sound more natural.Practise speaking with short syllables.Later, practise maintaining the energy on several syllables and longer sentences.



> Exercise for speaking in fuller metallic modes


Practise just how quiet and how loud you can speak in the fuller metallic modes on different vowels.Become familiar with these limits so that you will always be able to move safely within the modes.Take care not to speak too softly as you will lose the fuller metallic modes and change to Neutral.

Make sure you are not using Curbing and therefore trying to speak too loudly on Curbing vowels, as it can strain the voice and feel uncomfortable.If the volume is to exceed medium loud (mf), it is advisable to avoid Curbing vowels and replace them with Overdrive or Edge vowels.

Practise locating several different sound colours by combining the shape and position of the epiglottic funnel, larynx, tongue, mouth, palate and nasal passage.Locate the lightest and darkest sound colour in your voice and the colours in between.Choose the ones you like and practise speaking with these on both higher and lower notes.Notice the higher the pitch, the louder the volume possible.



> See ‘Sound Colour’


Recite a short text in the sound colours and volumes you prefer.Be careful not to speak so quietly that you loose the mode.

Practise speaking in the fuller metallic modes while combining different pitches, volumes, sound colours and vowels.
