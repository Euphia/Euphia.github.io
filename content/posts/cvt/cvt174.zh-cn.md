## The modes in classical singing
All singing across all genres and styles is performed with one of the four vocal modes as a basis.Even though classical singing sometimes gets a reputation for being a ‘special’ kind of singing distinct from other genres and styles, it is still the same modes being used, just with a specific chosen sound colour and stylistic way of phrasing.If you listen closely, you can identify all the same vowel changes describes in the modes and their use.Classical singing uses a particular sound colour across all the modes to make them sound classical and even across all pitches.

For example, if a female classical singer wishes to sing loudly (f-ff) in the low part of the voice, she could choose Overdrive or Edge depending on the vowels used and the character she wants.If she wishes to sing medium volume in the middle part of the voice, she could choose Edge or Curbing depending on which type of metallic character she wants.An Edge would have a clear metallic character and Curbing would have a restrained metallic character (notice that the metallic character will be disguised by the dark sound colour).In the high part of the voice, a female classical singer will use Neutral in all volumes with different amount of density, depending on the artistic expression.

If a male classical singer wishes to sing very loudly (f-ff) in the low or middle part of the voice, he will choose Overdrive or Edge depending on the vowels used and the character he wants.If he wishes to sing very loudly on a High C, he will usually choose Edge.If he wishes to sing medium volume in the middle part of the voice, he could choose Edge or Overdrive, if he wants a clear and open metallic character, and Curbing if he wants a restrained metallic character. (Again, notice that the metallic character will be disguised by the dark sound colour).He will use Neutral for singing quietly in all pitches.

If you are unaccustomed to the classical sound ideals, make sure you control each metallic vocal mode with their respective vowels, volumes, pitches, and sound colour possibilities before attempting to darken the metallic modes for a classical sound.Darkening the sound colour too much can make it more difficult to obtain the centres of the metallic modes and you may more quickly end up with uncontrolled constrictions.Remember to obey the three overall principles, establish the modes, and then alter the sound colour according to your artistic choice.
