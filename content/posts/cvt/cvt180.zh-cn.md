## Singing while dancing
### Dancers
Previously, dancers were often unfairly described as terrible singers.This was because dancers were taught to breathe with the upper part of the chest as dancing theory dictates that this is the correct way to breathe in order to perform physically demanding dances.However, this conflicted with the old view of singing that breathing should be abdominal.Dancers were reluctant to breathe in this way because they were taught that it would hinder their dancing.Besides, it did not follow the ideal of how a dancer should look.Also, dancers often have highly raised larynges (probably because of posture), which combined with their enormous physical strength often gives them a powerful fuller metallic voice, often in Edge.This distinctly metallic sound went against the traditional sound ideal and was therefore considered wrong.All this probably explains why dancers were wrongly labelled as terrible singers.Today, the obviously metallic sounds are much more accepted, and you often see dancers with leading roles in musical theatre shows, appreciated for their beautiful powerful voices.

### Singing and dancing at the same time
For singing, I would never recommend anyone to use an inhalation where the abdomen bulges out, as it is usually strenuous and uncomfortable.The pressure often triggers uncontrolled constriction and is not a particularly pretty sight either.Neither would I recommend an inhalation where the chest raises as this usually feels unpleasant and tight, is difficult to maintain and can create uncontrolled constriction and muscular tensions in the neck area.Instead, I urge dancers and others who sing to use an inhalation where the ribs expand as this benefit singing as well as dancing.

Make the diaphragm expand all the way around the chest, partly at the lower ribs and partly at the front in the solar plexus.This results in far greater expansion than when the chest rises and has the added advantage of being less visible than when the abdomen bulges.Thus, dancers only need to expand the chest slightly and need not fear that breathing will expand the abdomen.In my experience, many dancers who have altered their breathing in this way have benefited from the change.It is always better to follow the body than go against it.The body becomes stronger and is able to perform better in both singing and dancing.

Regarding sound, I would urge dancers and others to work on all the modes, in order to be able to achieve exactly the sound they prefer.It might be easier to start working on the metallic mode they lean most naturally toward.It is so physically demanding to dance and sing at the same time that it is important to use the centre of the mode to be the as efficient as possible and thereby get the most out of the invested energy.When singers and dancers are able to obtain the centre of the mode in their favourite metallic mode, they can move on to the other metallic modes.

Once they have achieved the centre of the metallic modes, the non-metallic mode (Neutral) becomes easier to find as they now have something to ‘avoid’, namely the metallic sound.When you control all the modes you can choose to sing in the mode you prefer.Do not worry if this is a metallic mode as the sound ideal has changed and today the metallic sounds are often preferred in musical theatre.

### 案例故事 Case Story
A very skilled saxophonist had become hoarse.He thought it was due to the way he spoke, but it turned out to be the result of uncontrolled constriction around the vocal cords from when he played his instrument.This was because he had been taught to use what in singing technique is referred to as ‘incorrect support’ while playing, i.e. the abdomen was pressed outwards during support.

The saxophonist was worried about altering his support technique after so many years, but he took the chance.When he started to follow the body instead of going against it, he could find much stronger energy for the support.Accordingly, he was able to reach louder volumes and higher notes on his instrument.Now, the uncontrolled constriction and his hoarseness disappeared.
