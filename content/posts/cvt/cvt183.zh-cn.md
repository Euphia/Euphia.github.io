## Solving a split in the voice
### A split in the voice
Some singers get a split in their voice from using the Neutral in falsetto.



> See ‘Neutral in falsetto’


> See ‘Training the very low part of the voice’


> See ‘Training the very high part of the voice’


Splitting is actually an unintentional Creaking.The voice will sound uneven and breathy and may sound as if two separate sounds are produced at the same time, as if the note is divided into two.



> Splitting


> See Video


If you continue singing with this splitting – maybe even purposely - it can worsen.In the end, it might be worked into your muscle memory to such a degree that you are able only to sing without splitting.If you have developed a split in your voice, you should immediately practise until it disappears.It can be very hard to get rid of a split.The longer the split is allowed to remain, the more distinct it can become and the harder it can be to remove.

If you wish to artistically use Creaking, make sure that you are in complete control of the Creaking and that you are, able to return to a healthy centre of the mode.Do not let Creaking become a habit that replaces your normal singing.

### Too small a sound or a break
If you sing in Neutral in falsetto, it is often a very quiet and thin sound.



> Neutral in falsetto below High C


Some singers wrongly think that they are practising a reduced or fuller density Neutral but instead they are using Neutral in falsetto.This may lead to some singing technique problems such as splitting and uncontrolled constriction.Therefore, it is important not to confuse the sounds of the fuller and reduced density Neutral with Neutral in falsetto.Singers should be aware whether they are singing in Neutral in falsetto or fuller and reduced density Neutral, in order avoid risk working in a split in the voice.



> Splitting


If you get used to singing with this split or break, getting rid of it can be very complicated and time-consuming.

So, using the Neutral in falsetto can cause problems:

- If you sing with Neutral in falsetto, you can be ‘caught’ in the thin and hollow sound and have difficulties getting out of the setting and singing louder.
- If you try to make that setting louder, a break or split in the voice could appear.
### 案例故事 Case Story
A pop singer with a great and powerful voice was annoyed that she always sang so loud.She contacted a singing teacher who told her she should practise singing as quiet as possible - with the tiniest, frail sounds as possible.But after practising this for some time her voice started to break every time she sang with more volume.The reason why she originally was singing so loudly was because she only sang in loud Overdrive and Edge.To sing quietly she should have practised Neutral in fuller & reduced density, but instead she had practised Neutral in falsetto, which had caused the problem.

We immediately started working in fuller & reduced density Neutral in order to obtain progressively quieter volumes without the risk of a break.To avoid the frail and thin sound (Neutral in falsetto), we trained with greater volumes.To avoid the metallic modes when the volume got louder in the high part, we at first practised exclusively on the vowels ‘OO’ (as in ‘you’) and ‘EE’ (as in ‘see’) taking care to keep her jaw relaxed; these precautions would ensure she stayed in Neutral.When she was able to sing in fuller & reduced density Neutral on ‘OO’ and ‘EE’, we proceeded to the other vowels.In the following weeks, it was important that she stayed in fuller & reduced density Neutral and did not sing so quietly that she ended up in Neutral in falsetto.After a month, she was able to control the Neutral mode with fuller & reduced density and was even able to sing quietly without breaking.Then, we worked on medium volume in Overdrive and Edge, so she could use all the volumes from quiet to medium loud.Any time she wanted louder volume, she could easily return to the loud Overdrive and Edge.

### Exercise for removing a split in your voice
Sing at such a powerful volume that the vocal cords simply CANNOT perform the Neutral in falsetto.This is usually easier in the lower part of the voice.Try to maintain a feeling of singing in fuller & reduced density Neutral in the various parts of your voice.If the split appears, increase your volume.Feel how you need to use higher support values than you normally would to avoid the split.When you are able to sing at a powerful volume without splitting, gradually decrease the volume while maintaining the feeling of singing in fuller & reduced density Neutral.Practise until you are able to sing quietly without a split.



> How to get rid of a split


### 案例故事 Case Story
A young rock singer had, by singing very quietly, found a split in her voice that she thought sounded good.She started practising it and used it a lot.After a while she found she could not get rid of it.It turned out that she had been singing in Neutral in falsetto.But when she tried to sing at a louder volume while maintaining Neutral in falsetto, the splitting sound appeared.Because she had used the split so much, it had become more or less permanent.

We started working on increasing the support energy and singing so loudly that she was not able to use Neutral in falsetto.In the beginning, she had to sing pretty loud to avoid the split.As her support energy improved and as she got used to the feeling of singing in fuller & reduced density Neutral, she was able to decrease the volume and still avoid the splitting.

After three weeks of practising with a clear voice and avoiding the splitting, she was again able to sing without the split.
