## 极高音区 Very high part of the voice
In this illustration, you will see the modes and volumes in the very high part of the voice for both men and women, above C6 for women, and above C5 for men.

In the illustration, you will see that men can use both Neutral (in fuller or reduced density and falsetto), Curbing and Edge in fuller & reduced density.Again, Neutral is used when a quiet to loud volume and a non-metallic character is wanted.Curbing or Edge in reduced density is used when a medium loud volume and restrained/contained character is wanted.Edge in fuller density is used when medium loud to loud volume, a fuller density and a clear and open character is wanted.Overdrive cannot be used in this part of the voice.

Women can only use Neutral above their high C.

Both sexes can use Neutral with audible air, but it is often quite difficult to achieve this at this pitch and you have to be careful not to trigger uncontrolled constriction.

In general, the very high part of the voice is only used for ornamentations or shorter passages.Long passages with lyrics are rare at this pitch.

On the coloured square bar in the right part of the illustration you will see that women can only sing in Neutral here, and men can also sing in Curbing and Edge.Overdrive cannot be sung in this part of the voice for neither women nor men.







### 方向而非绝对值 Directions rather than absolutes
“金属” 音和模式的平衡动态非常灵活，特别是对容错区的声音和模式转过程中的声音来说。你可以理解成，模式之间的边界是模糊的，因此在这些容错区，很难准确地确定声音处于哪一种模式。声音逐渐从一种模式变成另一种模式，平衡动态逐渐模糊，容错区逐渐扩大。这意味着这些插图中的各个区域只是对模式划分位置的粗略建议，而非确切边界。在这里，谈论声音的变化方向往往比试图寻找绝对性更有意义。

### 案例故事
A soul singer with an enormous voice had great power in the lower parts of her voice but only thin and delicate tones in the higher parts.It was difficult for her to complete her songs because she was unable to match these ‘two voices’.For years, teachers had warned her not to use the very powerful sounds in the low part of her voice and told her to thin her voice, ‘make her voice slimmer’ in order to ‘equalise the registers’.They also claimed that using such loud sounds in the low part of her voice was preventing her from reaching the higher parts of her voice.She therefore worked on ‘pulling down the frail top into the lower parts of the voice’ and ‘thinning’ her voice by practising with quieter volumes and less support.

I did not think that her problems were due to difficulties ‘equalising the registers’.I thought the problems were due to unsuitable uses of the modes and their densities.It turned out she sang in Overdrive with fuller density in the low part of the voice and in Neutral with falsetto in the high part.It was obvious why she did not have enough body in the sound in the high part of her voice.We worked on support, making it independent of pitch and volume.So instead of working at a quiet volume and limiting her big voice, which had of course frustrated her tremendously, we worked in loud volumes and fuller density.Here she could gain advantage of the considerable physical strength she had, and she could work directly on the problem, namely to free her large voice and not hold it back.

Once she could provide enough support energy on both high and low notes in fuller & reduced density Neutral, as well as singing quiet and loud volumes, she was ready to work with Edge.She located Edge by twanging the epiglottic funnel, using a sharp character and a light sound colour on the vowel ‘I’ (as in ‘sit’) and using large amounts of support energy and a loud volume.We practised until she could hit the centre of the Edge mode and as she was very strong, it did not take long before she was able to perform Edge to a G5.Now she had the loud sound all the way through her voice.

We could have stopped there, but she was very interested in working with the voice and her next wish was to sing quietly in the low part of her voice.We practised Neutral - again in fuller density - especially establishing the loose jaw and singing quietly with audible air using a large amount of support energy.Once she had been singing quietly through several songs, she became accustomed to how much support energy she needed to remain in Neutral.When she controlled Neutral, we then worked on changing between Edge and Neutral.

Now it almost became a sport for her to work with the voice, so we finally practised medium Overdrive and medium Edge so that she had a range of volume possibilities, between the medium and very loud Overdrive and Edge, and the quiet Neutral.Within three months, she had solved her technical problems by using the different modes and had obtained an enormous choice of sound colours and volumes.She now controlled the technique to such an extent that she was not only an outstanding singer, but she went on studying and later became a very skilled teacher.
