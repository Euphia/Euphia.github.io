## The shape of the mouth opening
Sound colour is produced among other places at level 6 (the rest of the vocal tract), when extending or relaxing the corners of the mouth.



See 'Various levels of the vocal tract – sound colour’



## Relaxing the corners of the mouth
The shape of the mouth also influences sound colour.If you sing with the corners of the mouth relaxed, you usually achieve a darker sound colour because the vocal tract becomes larger.

#### Relaxed corners of the mouth through images and sensations
You can also relax the corners of the mouth through images and sensations by:

- Imagining that you have been to the dentist and been anaesthetised so that you cannot move your lips
- Pretending to be stupid
- Looking like a kid watching TV
- Imagining that you have an egg in your mouth
