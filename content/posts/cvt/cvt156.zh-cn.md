## Byway 音量练习 Exercises in volume using Byway
如果选择使用低密态和听感克制的 Curbing，或听感抑制的低密态 Overdrive 和 Edge，则需要更频繁地更改模式，因为在 Curbing 或低密态 Overdrive 和 Edge，你无法唱得响亮（音量 7-10）。

以下练习侧重于在 Byway 保持均匀的音量歌唱。换句话说，在所有音量下，整个音域都使用低密态。Because the byway cannot be performed at loud volumes (volume 7-10) singing in the byway is limited to only sing at quiet, medium quiet and medium loud volumes.If you want to be loud in any part of the voice, you will need to make a change to the highway (a mode with fuller density).

### Exercises at a medium volume using Byway
If you want to go through all parts of the voice using reduced density and a restrained/contained character at a medium volume, you have to use several modes:

- begin in Overdrive in fuller or reduced density with a medium volume in the low part of the voice on the vowel ‘OH’ (as in ‘so’) or Edge in fuller or reduced density on the vowel ‘A’ (as in ‘and’).
- from the middle part of the voice change to Curbing (or reduced density Edge or reduced density Overdrive).Change the vowel towards ‘O’ (as in ’woman’) for Curbing, or to the contained vowel sounds in Overdrive and Edge in reduced density.Be sure to make a smooth transition to the reduced density and restrained/contained character if you started in fuller density Overdrive or Edge.
- continue as high as possible in Curbing or reduced density Edge or reduced density Overdrive. and when it becomes impossible to sing any higher without exceeding medium volume, change to fuller or reduced density Neutral, still on the vowel ‘O‘ or change to ‘OO’.
Practise starting in Edge in medium volume with the vowels ‘I’ (as in ‘sit’), ‘EH’ (as in ‘stay’), ‘A’ (as in ‘and’) and ‘OE’ (as in ‘herb’); or in Overdrive in medium volume on the vowel ‘EH’ (as in ‘stay’) or ‘OH’ (as in ‘so’); - change vowel to UH’ (as in ‘hungry’), ‘O’ (as in ‘woman’), or ‘OO’ (as in ‘you) for Curbing as you ascend; eventually change vowels again to ‘OO’ (as in ‘you’), ‘I’ (as in ‘sit’) ‘EE’ (as in ‘see’) and ‘AH’ (as in ‘far’) for Neutral in the high part.

Also practise the exercise the opposite way.Start in medium volume on a high note in fuller or reduced density Neutral on the vowel ‘O’, sing a descending scale in medium volume and change to Curbing or Overdrive and Edge in reduced density, and later change to Overdrive or Edge in fuller or reduced density.Make the transition as smooth as possible and practise altering the vowels gradually so there are no audible transitions or breaks.

## Byway exercise at a quiet volume
If you want to go through all parts of the voice at a quiet volume with a restrained or contained character, you should use Neutral with reduced density.Remember that the reduced density Neutral may be difficult to perform in the low part of the voice.

Use Neutral in reduced density at a quiet volume all the way up through the range to achieve singing softly and quietly in all ranges.You may have to either accept that the density increases slightly in the low part of the voice or that the volume decreases slightly when staying in reduced density Neutral in such a low pitch where the setting is not the easiest to perform.

Be aware, in the high part of the voice that you do not unintentionally change to Neutral in falsetto and that you in the low part of the voice do not unintentionally change to Neutral in fuller density.If it is difficult avoiding the Neutral in fuller density or Neutral In falsetto, make sure to have a clear sensation of the hold throughout the range, letting the volume diminish slightly in the low part and increase slightly in the high part.Once you get used to singing reduced density Neutral across all ranges, you can make the volume more even.

Notice how you have to increase your support energy in the low and high part of the voice to maintain the mode.Sing the ascending scale beginning with a good amount of support energy but making sure you have enough support energy left for the highest notes.

Be aware that you can use many different sound colours in Neutral, but make sure the sound colour and volume do not change during the exercise.Practise all vowels this way so you establish muscle memory for singing quietly in all pitches across a range of sound colours.

You can also practise Neutral with audible air all the way up through the voice.Try adding audible air to the reduced density Neutral and notice the amount of extra support energy required to maintain the audible air in the high part of the voice.Be sure to keep the same density as you go up in pitch.Practise all vowels this way until you have trained adding audible air to Neutral with reduced density in all pitches.

### Increase the volume (crescendo) using Byway.
Sing with an increasingly powerful volume in reduced density and a restrained/contained character as you go up through the range of your voice.Begin at a quiet volume and increase the volume until you are singing very powerfully (‘ff’ or ‘fortissimo’) in the high part:

- start with a quiet volume in the low part of the voice in fuller or reduced density Neutral and add audible air on the vowel ‘OO’ (as in ‘you’).
- increase the volume and remove the air
- change to Curbing in the middle part of the voice on the vowel ‘UH’. (or Overdrive/Edge in reduced density on the vowel ‘Eh’ as in ‘stay’).
- continue to increase the volume and change to Edge on the vowel ‘A’ (as in ‘and’).Be sure to make a smooth transition to the fuller density and clear and open character.

Also practise the exercise the opposite way.Start in loud volume in Edge on a high note on the vowel ‘A’ and decrease the volume during a descending scale, change to Curbing on the vowel ‘UH’ and change to Neutral on the vowel ‘OO’.Make the transitions as smooth as possible and practise altering the vowels gradually so that the changes become inaudible

### Decrease the volume (decrescendo) using Byway
Sing gradually quieter with reduced density and a restrained/contained character as you go up through the range of your voice.Begin with a powerful volume and gradually decrease it until you are singing very quietly (‘pp’ or ‘pianissimo’) in the high part:

- start with a powerful volume in the low part in Overdrive on the vowel ‘EH’ (as in ‘stay’), perhaps with a lowered larynx.
- decrease the volume in the middle part of the voice and change to Curbing (or Overdrive/Edge in reduced density) and change to the vowel to ‘UH’ (as in ‘hungry’) or to the contained vowel sounds in Overdrive and Edge in reduced density.Be sure to make a smooth transition to the reduced density and restrained/contained character.
- continue to decrease the volume and sing in Neutral with reduced density and a restrained character in the high part of the voice, still on the vowel ‘UH’
- continue to decrease the volume and as you go higher, gradually change to Neutral, still with reduced density and a restrained character, while adding audible air to the voice in the high part of the voice.Perhaps alter the vowel to ‘AH’.


> Exercise for decrescendo, byway





Also practise the exercise the opposite way.Start in a quiet volume on a high note in Neutral with audible air on the vowel ‘AH’, sing a descending scale, remove the air, increase the volume and change to reduced density Neutral, then Curbing (or Overdrive/Edge in reduced density), and end in Overdrive on the vowel ‘EH’ in very loud volume.If you want a sharper sound, you can also end the exercise in fuller density Edge at a loud volume on the vowel ‘EH’ (as in ‘stay’) or ‘A’ (as in ‘And’).Make the transitions as smooth as possible and practise altering the vowels gradually in order to avoid audible changes or breaks.
