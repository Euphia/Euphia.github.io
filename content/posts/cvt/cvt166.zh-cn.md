## 中等响亮的音量 medium volume
如果想在全音域实现中等响亮音量，你有很多可能的选择。

### Highway using metal
If you want to sing medium volume with fuller density and a clear and open character up through your range, you can use Overdrive or Edge.Be careful not to sing too high in pitch for Overdrive, so change to Edge for the high and very high part of the voice.



> Exercise at medium volume, highway using metal





### Highway using Neutral
Perhaps you want to keep the fuller density and medium volume but change to Neutral in the high part.If so, you must obey the rules for Overdrive and Edge in fuller density with the clear and open character, in order to stay in this density and volume up to the high part of the voice, and from here change to Neutral in fuller density.



Exercise at medium volume, highway using Neutral





### Byway using metal
If you want to sing in medium volume with reduced density and a contained character, you can use reduced density in Overdrive up to the pitch limit, or Edge in your entire range.Be careful not to sing too high in pitch for Overdrive, so change to Curbing or Edge with reduced density for the high and very high part of the voice.



> Exercise at medium volume, byway with reduced density Overdrive


> Exercise at medium volume, byway with reduced density Edge





Perhaps you want the sound of the restrained character from Curbing.Then you can use Overdrive or Edge in the low part and change to Curbing in the middle part of the voice and from there continue upwards in Curbing or Edge.



> Exercise at medium volume, byway using Curbing





### Byway using Neutral
Perhaps you want the sound of reduced density and the contained, held-in character from reduced Overdrive or reduced Edge.Then you can use reduced Overdrive or Edge in the low part and middle part of the voice, and from there change to Neutral in the high part of the voice.

如果你想唱出低密度声音，可以使用低密态 Neutral。

如果你想唱出高密度声音，则可以使用高密态 Neutral。

如果你想唱假声，你可以使用假声 Neutral。



> Exercise at medium volume, byway using Neutral
